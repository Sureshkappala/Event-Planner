Frequently Inquired Questions

How far in advance should we book?
For large-scale weddings and summits (200+ attendees), we recommend reserving 6 to 12 months in advance. For private soirees, 4 to 8 weeks is typically sufficient.
Do you coordinate destination celebrations outside the US?
Yes, our team routinely orchestrates destination galas across Lake Como, Paris, the Amalfi Coast, Kyoto, and the Caribbean islands.
Can we customize our package inclusions?
Every package serves as a refined baseline. We routinely tailor bespoke floral sculptors, celebrity entertainment, and sommelier menus.
