Venue Architecture

Spatial Floorplan & Immersive Zones

Engineered for effortless guest movement, intimate seating clusters, and dramatic stage visibility.

Grand Imperial Ballroom (350 Seats)

Circular banquet tables dressed in champagne velvet with central 8-foot crystal floral candelabras and 360° sightlines.

VIP Mezzanine & Cigar Lounge

Exclusive upper terrace featuring plush Italian leather lounges, vintage scotch bar, and private balcony overlooking the dancefloor.

The Belvedere Glasshouse Promenade

Guest Etiquette & Arrival Protocol
