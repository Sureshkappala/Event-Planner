Our Genesis

Elevating Event Planning to an Art Form

Stackly was established in 2014 by a collective of luxury hoteliers, set designers, and floral botanists in Manhattan. Our ethos is simple: no two celebrations should ever feel alike.

From the subtle acoustic harmony of a string quartet in an Italian courtyard to the grandeur of a multi-tiered gala ballroom with custom crystal chandeliers, we curate every second so hosts can be truly present with their guests.

Our Guiding Principles

The Four Pillars of Stackly Excellence

Every gala, royal wedding, and private summit we design is anchored by an uncompromising dedication to bespoke craftsmanship.

Sensory Spatial Architecture

We do not merely decorate ballrooms; we architect immersive sensory journeys encompassing lighting color temperature, custom acoustic balancing, and spatial flow.

Botanical & Floral Mastery

Our floral ateliers import rare seasonal blooms directly from sustainable growers in Holland, Ecuador, and Kyoto, creating live organic sculptures.

Absolute VIP Discretion
